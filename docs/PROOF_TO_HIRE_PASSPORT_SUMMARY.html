TALENTIQ · HACKATHON PRODUCT SUMMARY

Proof-to-Hire Passport

USP: TalentIQ turns scattered work into a role-specific, explainable hiring brief that shows what a candidate can do, why the evidence is credible, and what risk remains.

Why it is different: Rather than a black-box talent score or resume keyword match, the Passport links every competency decision to independently collected evidence from code, hackathons, presentations, team delivery, and interviews.

What the recruiter sees

  • Role readiness, evidence confidence, and explicit trust risk.
  • A competency proof graph with source-linked evidence cards.
  • Uncertainty and verification gaps instead of invented certainty.
  • The next best targeted interview question to close the most important gap.
  • Candidate consent and auditability messaging.

Scale and business path

Process GitHub, hackathon, upload, and interview evidence asynchronously through connector adapters and queues. Store immutable provenance, source hashes, timestamps, consent, and scoring versions. Begin with PostgreSQL, object storage, Redis queues, and pgvector retrieval; add graph infrastructure only when relationship volume proves the need.

Go to market through hackathon organizers and campuses: event SaaS creates a consented hiring pool, recruiter subscriptions monetize access, and enterprise ATS/private-pool integrations expand the product.
